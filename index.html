💡 Shopping Tips
🏷️
Always ask for BIS-certified fertilizers and licensed pesticides from a registered dealer.
📋
Carry your soil test report to get the exact grade (e.g. 10-26-26 NPK) recommended for your crop.
⚖️
Buy in exact quantities — overstocking fertilizers reduces their effectiveness over time.
🏦
Check for PM-KISAN and State government subsidy cards — many shops accept them for discounts.
